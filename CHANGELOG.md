# Changelog
All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added
- Seven coaster shapes: round, square, hexagon, octagon, flower, scallop and gear, with an optional rim.
- Artwork from 84 quick picks, a library of thousands of icons and brand logos, or your own SVG, PNG or JPG. Images are traced automatically; you can adjust the threshold.
- Sketchpad: draw your own design with pen and eraser, with mirror, radial or kaleidoscope symmetry.
- Curved text along the top and bottom of the rim, center text and eight fonts.
- Seven patterns (rings, dots, honeycomb, waves, sunburst, checker, terrazzo) that keep clear of your artwork and text.
- Color themes and filament colors for the preview.
- Raised, engraved or flush inlaid designs, face-down printing, and a cork recess or pockets for rubber feet on the underside.
- Community gallery: share your coaster, browse, search, like, report and remix designs, with share links.
- "Surprise me" for a random coaster.
- A "Mug" toggle in the 3D view toolbar (off by default) that puts a typical coffee mug on the coaster, so you can see whether it is big enough.
- Moderation page at /admin (guarded by ADMIN_TOKEN): review reported and hidden designs, read report reasons, dismiss reports, hide, restore and delete designs.
